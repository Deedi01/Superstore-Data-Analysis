# Superstore-Data-Analysis

## Business objective
  To analyze the superstore's sales and profit data in order to understand overall performance and identify areas that drive profitability, so the management can make better decisions about growth.

## Business Questions
- What is the overall sales performance of the business? 
- Which regions generated the highest sales and profit? 
- Which customer segment has the hight sales contribution? 
- What product category performed the best? 
- What are the most profitable products? 
- What trends can be observed overtime? 

## Data
Dataset with 5000 orders record. Original source; Kaggle.

## Tools
 - **Power BI** (Power query, DAX, Data Visualization)

## Key Metrics
- **Total Sales:** $2.30M
- **Total Orders:** 5000
- **Average Order Value:** $458.61
- **Total Profit:** $286.40K
- **Profit Margin:** 12.47%

## Key Findings
-	The West Region is the strongest-performing region with about $725k in sales and $108k in profit. This indicates that the West Region has a strong customer base and effective sales performance.
-	Technology is evidently the highest-performing category with about $836k in sales. With Furniture ($742k) and Office Supplies ($719k) trailing behind.
- Consumer customers generated the highest revenue in the business at about $1.16M, more than 50% of the total revenue. Corporate consumers generated $706K and Home office made the least sales at $430K.
- High discount levels negatively affect profitability. The company generates profit with little or no discounting, but medium and high discounts lead to losses.
- Sales increase significantly in the final quarter, with November recording the highest monthly sales ($272k). Although September ($249K) and December ($249K) are not so far behind

## Recommendations
- Reduce High-Discount Promotions
Since the medium and high discount bands generate losses, management should implement discount limits and require approval for aggressive discounting.
- Invest More in Technology Products
Technology is the highest-selling category and contributes significantly to total revenue. Investing more in this category will lead to Increased sales growth and stronger market positioning.
-	Focus Marketing Efforts on the West and East Regions
These regions produce the highest sales and profits. Management should increase advertisement in those areas, as it could lead to higher return on marketing investment and increased revenue growth.
- Investigate Profitability Issues in the South and Central Regions
Management should review product mix, pricing strategies, shipping costs, and discount policies in these regions.
- Maximize Year-End Sales Opportunities
Sales are strongest during the final quarter of the year, especially in November. Management should launch major promotional campaigns, inventory planning, and customer engagement initiatives before the holiday season to maximize revenue opportunities.
- Grow Corporate and Home Office Segments
The business currently relies heavily on consumer customers. Management should develop targeted campaigns and loyalty programs for Corporate and Home Office customers to diversify revenue sources and reduce dependence on a single segment.
